How Does Our Mobile Welding Work Beyond New Orleans?

Our mobile welding works the same in every city we serve. You request a free quote, we book a time that suits you, and our welders drive a fully equipped mobile unit to your property. The weld happens on site, so you never haul a gate, a railing or a length of pipe to a shop.

What Do Customers Ask About Our New Orleans Welding Service Areas?

Answers to the questions we hear most about which cities our mobile welders reach, from the Northshore to Baton Rouge, whether we work at businesses as well as homes, whether estimates stay free outside the city and what to do if your town is not on the list yet.

Do you serve the Northshore?

Yes. Covington, Mandeville, Madisonville and Slidell are all on our service area list, and each has its own page with local details. Our mobile units drive across the lake to your property for the same on-site welding we offer in New Orleans.

Do you work in Baton Rouge and Hammond?

Yes. Baton Rouge and Hammond are both on our service area list. Request a free estimate with your address, and we will schedule a time for our welders to bring the rig to you.

What if my city is not on the list?

Request a free estimate or call us at (504) 370-2757 with your address. We will confirm whether we can reach you before we book the job, so you get a clear answer either way.

How far will your mobile welders travel from New Orleans?

Our service area runs west to Baton Rouge, east to Slidell and north across Lake Pontchartrain to Covington, Mandeville, Madisonville and Hammond. If your property sits outside those cities, request a free estimate and we will confirm before we book the job.

Does every city get the same services?

Yes. Every city on our list can book the full range of our services, from pipe, fence, gate, railing and window welding to arc welding, custom welding and metal fabrication, all done on site by our licensed and insured welders.
